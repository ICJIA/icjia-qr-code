Most modern devices can scan a QR code with the built-in camera &mdash; no separate app required.

## iPhone

- Open the **Camera** app.
- Point it at the QR code.
- Tap the notification banner that appears to open the link.
- Works on iOS 11 and later.

## Android

- Recent phones: open the built-in **Camera** app and point it at the code.
- **Google Lens** is available through Google Assistant or as a standalone app.
- Otherwise, install a QR code scanner from the Play Store.

## Other devices

- Most tablet cameras support QR scanning.
- Desktop and laptop users can use a webcam with a browser-based reader.
- Many banking and payment apps include a built-in scanner.

Before you publish a code, follow the [best practices](/best-practices/) and always test it on a real device.
